## Evidence and deliverables

The research paper and executive summary are finalized using the credited public Maven Analytics dataset. Maven Roasters is fictional and the records cover 2023-01-01 through 2023-06-30. The public-data case study is complete; these are not actual Afficionado or 2025 sales findings.

The paper covers provenance, validation, methodology, EDA, product/category/store findings, concentration, recommendations and limitations. Numerical tables derive from the reproducible analysis runner. The original workbook, prepared CSV and analytical summaries are included in the local submission outputs. The licensed compressed public CSV is bundled in the repository.

All 18 unit/application tests pass. An additional full-source application check verifies five dashboard views and the Astoria revenue filter. Overall, per-product and dimension totals reconcile to independent controls. Browser checks verify populated case-study charts and visible source attribution.

Streamlit uses dashboard/app.py and Python 3.12. Vercel root is website/, preset Other, no build command. Private uploaded data is not published.
