# Step 7: revenue concentration and menu balance

Added src/concentration.py and integrated its results into the Streamlit dashboard.

The Pareto chart displays individual product revenue and cumulative revenue percentage across every observed product in the selected filter scope. Revenue arithmetic and threshold comparisons use Decimal values from validated transaction rows.

Products needed for 80% = minimum count in descending revenue order whose cumulative revenue reaches or exceeds 80%. The crossing product is included. Equal revenue is ordered by product ID; a warning identifies a tie across the boundary. Revenue anchors are that minimum set. Long-tail products are the remaining observed products, with their individual shares and combined contribution displayed.

CR-N measures top-N revenue divided by full filtered revenue. The sidebar Top-N slider affects CR-N only, leaving the Pareto population intact. The dashboard also reports revenue generated by the top 20% of observed products, rounding the product count upward; it does not assume an 80/20 relationship.

The long tail is a review group, not an automatic menu removal list. Filters can materially alter concentration, and sold-product coverage is not a complete menu catalog. Costs, inventory availability, and strategic role remain necessary for stronger business recommendations.
